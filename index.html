01 / About

Engineering with context.

I work at the intersection of complex networks, practical troubleshooting and the systems that can make operations more proactive.

From reactive
to predictive.

A guiding idea across my engineering work and research.

In network engineering, an outage is often the final visible moment of a much longer story. Small changes in telemetry, control-plane behavior and system health can reveal that story earlier—if we know how to look.

My work involves investigating service-provider network issues, reproducing faults in the lab and collaborating toward verified fixes. I’m also interested in applying machine learning to time-series data and making network automation safer and more predictable.
