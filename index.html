About

Every day I take a broken network, work out why it broke, reproduce it in the lab, and help engineering ship the fix. After two years of that, I’ve learned that the warning signs are usually there before the call comes in.

My research started with the same idea. After the 2023 Türkiye earthquake, I began predicting earthquakes from seismic time series. I’m now exploring how the same thinking applies to network telemetry, and how to make network automation and AI agents safe to run on critical infrastructure.
